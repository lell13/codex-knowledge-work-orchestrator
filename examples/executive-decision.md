# Example: executive decision

```text
Use $knowledge-work-orchestrator.

Compare the three attached proposals and prepare a one-page recommendation
for the executive committee. Show total cost, implementation risk, important
assumptions, and the strongest reason not to choose your recommendation.

Do not send the memo or accept any proposal.
```

Expected routing: Assurance mode. Luna extracts comparable facts, Terra performs the decision analysis, the root drafts the memo, and Sol reviews material reasoning or omitted risk before final integration.
