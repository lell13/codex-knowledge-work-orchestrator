# Example: market research brief

```text
Use $knowledge-work-orchestrator.

Research the current Brazilian market for practical AI training for small
business owners. Produce a concise decision memo for a solo founder.

Requirements:
- use current primary or authoritative sources where possible
- separate facts, inferences, and open questions
- compare no more than three viable customer segments
- recommend one next experiment
- do not contact anyone or purchase data
```

Expected routing: Team mode with one Luna researcher. Add a Terra analyst only if the evidence requires a separate comparison pass. Sol review is unnecessary unless the memo will support a material investment.
